NOVABALL — hover-car football at the edge of the galaxy

NOVABALL is a free 3D car-soccer game that runs in your web browser. Drive a mag-lev hover car, boost, jump, dodge-flip and ride the force-field walls to strike the glowing Nova Core into the rival goal, in a holographic stadium floating above a ringed gas giant. No download, no sign-up and no wallet needed to play.

Game features

  • 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 matches against AI bots at Rookie, Pro and Legend difficulty, plus a Free Play mode.
  • Short 1-, 2- or 4-minute matches with sudden-death overtime.
  • Core Charge: each consecutive touch by your team charges the ball, so one goal can be worth up to 3 points (SUPERNOVA).
  • NOVA PULSE: a radial shockwave that blasts the ball and shoves nearby rivals.
  • ZERO-G SURGE: every minute the stadium drops to low gravity for ten seconds of aerial chaos.
  • Goal slow-motion, instant replays, demolitions, boost pads and a procedural synthwave soundtrack.

Play to earn on Arbitrum

After a match, claim your match points as NOVA tokens (ERC-20, 1 NOVA per point) with one transaction from MetaMask, Brave Wallet, Phantom or any EIP-6963 browser wallet. Spend NOVA in the garage on NFT car bodies and paints (ERC-1155 with on-chain SVG art), then drive them in your next match. Every claim records the match result on-chain, and career stats and the leaderboard are read live from the blockchain. Fast blocks and sub-cent fees on Arbitrum make a transaction per match practical.

How to play

Keyboard and mouse: W / S or the arrow keys to drive, A / D to steer, Space or right mouse to jump and dodge, Shift or left mouse to boost, C to powerslide, Q / E to air roll, F for NOVA PULSE, Tab for ball cam and Esc to pause. Standard gamepads are supported too.

Frequently asked questions

Is NOVABALL free to play?

Yes. The whole game is free in the browser; a wallet is only needed to claim NOVA rewards and buy NFT cosmetics.

What do I need to run it?

A desktop browser with WebGL 2: the latest Chrome, Edge or Firefox, or Safari 16.4 and newer, with hardware acceleration enabled.

Which blockchain does NOVABALL use?

Arbitrum (currently the Arbitrum Sepolia testnet). Gas for a claim or a purchase is paid in ETH and costs a fraction of a cent.
